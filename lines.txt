not accumulation.

it already controls you.

not effort.

Comfort is the most expensive habit.

Being “nice” is how people
avoid being powerful.

Hard work doesn’t make you rich.
Leverage does.

If you chase quick money,
you’ll stay broke.

when you’re addicted to comfort.

Most people don’t fail from laziness.
